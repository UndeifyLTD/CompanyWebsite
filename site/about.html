About Undeify

Last updated 16 October 2026

Undeify is a product company, not an agency. We pick problems we understand, build software for them, and stay to run and improve it long after launch.

Who we are

Undeify was started by Isaac and Tolu, who design, build and support everything themselves. Isaac leads the company and its security; Tolu leads development. The people who wrote the code are the people who answer your email.

We are completing our registration with the Corporate Affairs Commission. Our registered name and number will appear on this page once it is done.

How we work

  • Our own products. We do not build websites or apps for clients. Everything we make carries our name, so we decide what goes in, what stays out, and when it is ready.
  • Security from the start. We ask how each feature could be abused before we build it. Secrets stay out of our code, and every service gets only the access it needs.
  • Built for Nigerian law. We design every product around the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: collect only what a feature needs, say plainly why, and keep it no longer than necessary.

What we are building

  • NOW, a social map of places, starting with a private beta in Lagos. In design.
  • ShiftOS, scheduling and operations for teams that work in shifts. Running in production.

In research

We are researching an app for learning Yorùbá, made for families who live abroad, starting with parents who want to learn it themselves. It is on hold while NOW comes first, and we will build it only once people tell us they need it.
