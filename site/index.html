How we build

security.txt

Security from the start

We ask how each feature could be abused before we build it, not after. Secrets stay out of our code, every service gets only the access it needs, and anyone can report a problem.

NDPA

Built for Nigerian law

Every product is designed around the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: collect only what a feature needs, say why, and keep it no longer than necessary.

2

Small on purpose

Two founders design, build and support everything. The people who wrote the code answer your email.
