Every product starts as a problem worth a few late nights.

We only build for problems we understand, most of them from everyday life in Nigeria.

Then we build it, test it with real people, and keep improving it.

Nothing ships until we have asked how it could be abused, and fixed the answer.

Undeify is a product company, not an agency. We pick problems we understand, build software for them, and stay to run and improve it long after launch.

Our own products. We do not build websites or apps for clients. Everything we make carries our name, so we decide what goes in, what stays out, and when it is ready. Our first products serve two very different people: someone choosing where to go tonight in Lagos, and a manager planning next week's shifts.

Security from the start. We ask how each feature could be abused before we build it, not after. Secrets stay out of our code, and every service gets only the access it needs. When we find a weakness, we fix it and write down what we learned, so the next product starts safer than the last.

Built for Nigerian law. We design every product around the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: collect only what a feature needs, say plainly why, and keep it no longer than necessary. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no tracking of any kind.

Small on purpose. Undeify is run by its two founders, who design, build and support everything themselves. Decisions are quick, and the people who wrote the code are the people who answer your email. We will grow the team when the products need it, not before.
