Privacy notice

Last updated 16 October 2026

This notice explains how Undeify handles personal data on this website, undeify.com. Each of our products has its own privacy notice, shown before that product collects anything.

Who we are

Undeify is a software company in Nigeria. We are completing our registration with the Corporate Affairs Commission, and our registered name and number will be added here when it is done. For anything about your data, write to hello@undeify.com.

What this website collects

Very little. This website has no forms, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising or tracking scripts. Our fonts and images are served from our own site, not from third parties.

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, receives the technical details every website receives: your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. Cloudflare uses them to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. We do not use them to identify or profile visitors.
  • Emails you send us. If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and your message. We use them only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.

Why we may use it

We rely on our legitimate interest in running a secure website for server logs, and on your request for emails you send us. We never sell personal data, and we do not use it for marketing unless you have asked us to.

How long we keep it

Hosting logs are kept by Cloudflare for the short period its service needs. We keep emails for as long as the conversation is active and for up to two years afterwards, then delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Where it is processed

Cloudflare and our email provider may process data outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 allows for transfers abroad.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to stop or limit how we use it, and to give you a copy. Write to hello@undeify.com and we will answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
